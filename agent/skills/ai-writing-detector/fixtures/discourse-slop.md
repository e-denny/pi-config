Three conditions are built into the word, and the order of the three is part of the
argument. What is the substance of value? Labour. What is its measure? Labour time.
The distinction is not academic, and the word it needs saying plainly carries the
weight.

Marx's description of the moment is worth keeping. The formula contains a scandal,
and the next chapter is a response to it. The break is easy to mistake for a
technicality.

Against the first: value is a relation. Against the second: profit is a claim on
surplus labour. Against the third: crisis is endogenous to accumulation.

Strip away everything that distinguishes them, their usefulness, their material
properties, the craft that made each, and what remains is human labour in general.

Press on the profit and the ready-made answers arrive at once. Exploitation here is
a structural description of a contract, not a moral accusation. It is the return for
risk. It is payment for waiting. It is the mark-up the shop adds to what it bought.
Accumulation is the normal case, not an exception.
